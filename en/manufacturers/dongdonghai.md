# DongDongHai (DDH)
**DongDongHai** (DDH) is a Chinese maker of flashlight hosts: bodies with a head, reflector, battery tube and switch, sold without emitter or driver for DIY builds. They are sold mainly on Taobao, and some are rebadged by Kaidomain. Enthusiasts call them "Dongs".

*Part of [Manufacturers](/manufacturers).*

### User interfaces
XBY drivers come with one of two UIs, and on drivers that have both, 7H switches between them:
- [XBY original UI](/user-interfaces/xby-original-ui): the older stock UI
- [Technoman UI](/user-interfaces/technoman-ui): the newer custom UI, which adds moon modes, buck-only turbo limits (14C) and rearranged controls

The INeedMoreLumens drivers run Anduril 2.

To install the Technoman UI on an XBY47 v4.2 driver, see [Flashing the XBY47 v4.2 driver](/guides/flashing-xby47-v4-2).

## Notes
- DDH reflectors are reported to be interchangeable with those of the Convoy C8, C8+, M21A and MantaRay M45.
- Kaidomain rebrands: K1C = M1C, K2C = M2C, K2C-T = M2C Triple, K2G = D2G, K5R = D2i.
