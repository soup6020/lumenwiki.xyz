# User interfaces
Driver firmware user interfaces (UIs), meaning the click and hold patterns that control a light.

## Notation
| Notation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **1C**, **2C**, … | Click the button once, twice, and so on, in quick succession |
| **1H** | Press and hold |
| **3H** | Click twice, then press and hold on the third press |
